Privacy Policy

Piki & Max · Effective October 12, 2026

In short: Piki & Max does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. Your progress and settings stay on your device.

This policy explains how the game Piki & Max (the “App”), made by Black Yellow Studio (“we”, “us”), handles information. If you have any questions, write to juancarlos@blackyellow.studio.

Information we do not collect

The App does not ask for, collect or send us any personal information. In particular, it does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, voice, device or advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. The App contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code, and it does not use the internet to talk to any server.

Information stored only on your device

To work properly, the App saves a few things on your iPhone or iPad. This information never leaves your device and we cannot see it:

  • Game progress and records (unlocked levels, high scores, best times, coins found).
  • Settings (difficulty, language, music and sound volume, vibration, screen shake, controls feel).
  • Whether you have already seen the story and the tips.
  • A random nickname such as “Player 123”, used only for nearby co-op (see below).

Deleting the App deletes all of this. Like other app data, it may be included in your device backups (for example iCloud Backup), which are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

Nearby co-op (two iPhones)

The App can connect directly to a friend's iPhone nearby so you can play together. For this, iOS will ask your permission to use the local network. When you play this way, the two devices exchange only what the game needs: the random nickname, which sibling each player chose, button presses and the state of the game. This information goes straight from one device to the other over Wi-Fi or peer-to-peer Wi-Fi; it is not sent over the internet, it is not sent to us, and it is not saved after the session. You can turn local network access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Game controllers

The App uses Apple's Game Controller framework to read buttons from controllers that iOS recognizes. Controller input is used only to play and is not stored or shared.

Information Apple may share with us

The App is distributed through Apple's App Store. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous information such as crash reports and the number of downloads. This information does not identify you. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Piki & Max is made for families and children. Because the App collects no personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) or children under 16 in the European Union. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question, please contact us at juancarlos@blackyellow.studio.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law) you may have rights to access or delete personal information about you. Since we do not collect or hold any personal information from the App, there is nothing for us to access or delete; you can remove all App data by deleting the App. If you email us, we will use your email only to reply to you and will delete it on request.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date above before the change is released.
